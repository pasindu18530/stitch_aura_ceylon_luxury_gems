## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the quiet authority, provenance, and tactile perfection of high jewelry and museum-grade Ceylon gemstones. The aesthetic balances contemporary editorial restraint with classical lapidary heritage. Every viewport functions like a private viewing room in Mayfair or Place Vendôme: generous negative space, uncompromising typographic discipline, and subtle material warmth that eliminates visual noise so raw gemstone optical qualities take center stage.

The emotional signature is poise, heirloom permanence, and cryptographic authenticity. The design movement is Modern Minimalist Editorial infused with Tactile Luxury—relying on hairline structural grid lines, warm luminous alabaster canvases, and deliberate micro-interactions that mirror the deliberate handling of archival stones.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the geological and historical legacy of Sri Lankan gemstone mining—alluvial gravels, unheated sapphire depths, and refined precious metals.

- **Primary (`#141413`)**: Obsidian Charcoal. Replaces harsh synthetic black with an organic, ink-rich tone for typography, high-contrast structural dividers, and dominant call-to-action surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted Champagne Gold. A disciplined, non-glitzy metallic accent for provenance stamps, active interactive states, fine linear ornaments, and certification ribbons.
- **Tertiary (`#0D2240`)**: Royal Ceylon Sapphire. Reserved strictly for prestigious focal points: certified unheated sapphire disclosures, high-security authentication badges (Gübelin, SSEF), and bespoke atelier VIP actions.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF8F5`)**: Alabaster Silk. A warm, illuminated off-white surface that mitigates glare while honoring diamond and sapphire color grading.
- **Neutral Elevated Tier (`#F3EEEA`)**: Parchment Beige. Used for inset vault trays, interactive filters, drawer containers, and micro-data backdrops.
- **Subtle Stroke Token (`#E8E2DA`)**: A whisper-thin boundary token providing precise architectural boundaries without visual weight.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is built upon a deliberate contrast between classical Roman editorial elegance and surgical modern data presentation.

- **Display & Headline Levels (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys museum-grade authority, editorial prestige, and romantic legacy. Display and headline titles should remain light to medium in weight, emphasizing graceful ascenders, sweeping ligatures, and natural optical rhythm.
- **Body & Scientific Micro-Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crystalline legibility for technical gemological reports, carat metrics, and multi-currency pricing. 
- **Labels & Certificates (`Plus Jakarta Sans` in uppercase)**: Set with expanded letter-spacing (from `0.12em` to `0.16em`) to reflect cold, architectural hallmarks stamped into platinum and gold ingots.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors physical luxury publishing: wide margins, structural vertical rules, and intentional spatial breath.

- **Desktop (1440px and above)**: 12-column symmetrical grid with `margin: 4rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Gallery viewports and master gemstone showcases utilize asymmetrical column spanning (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for provenance documentation).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column product and gem card configurations.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Full-bleed inspection views for stones, transitioning horizontal technical data grids into stacked bespoke accordion registers.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Controlled strictly through the `space-*` scale. Component internals rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for high-density micro-data, while section gaps deliberately expand to `space-xl` and multi-token compounds to maintain an unhurried, gallery-grade pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through tactile surface stacking and subtle ambient refraction rather than heavy directional drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**: Base tier sits at `#FAF8F5`. Interactive cards and floating trays elevate to pure optical white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted silk parchment (`#F3EEEA`), framed strictly by razor-sharp 1px borders in `#E8E2DA`.
- **Ambient Shadows**: When elevation is required for floating inspection loupes, private viewing drawers, or modal vaults, shadows are rendered as extra-diffused, dual-layer ambient veils tinted with obsidian and warm gold:
  - *Floating Jewel Card*: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.08)`
  - *Vault Modal & Drawer*: `0 24px 60px -12px rgba(13, 34, 64, 0.08), 0 4px 16px 0 rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.03)`
- **Glassmorphic Loupe Surfaces**: Interactive 360-degree controls and certification inspection badges use `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.75)` with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter and an inset `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined architectural corner language (`roundedness: 1`), honoring the emerald cut and step-cut facets typical of high-carat Ceylon sapphires.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem`)**: Applied to gemstone cards, certificates, data plaques, and input elements. This micro-bevel preserves the precision of hand-cut gems while softening digital edge harshness.
- **Pill Exceptions (`rounded-full`)**: Reserved exclusively for dynamic status chips (e.g., "Unheated", "SSEF Certified", "In Vault"), interactive 360-degree rotation indicators, and tactile audio-visual loupe toggles.
- **Internal Borders**: All cards and architectural sections use a uniform hairline stroke width (`1px`) with zero miter distortion.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Atelier Button**: Solid `#141413` with `#FAF8F5` text, uppercase `label-md` tracking, 14px vertical padding, 28px horizontal padding, and a transition to `#0D2240` on hover with a 1px border highlight in `#C5A880`.
- **Secondary Provenance Button**: Transparent background enclosed in a 1px `#141413` hairline border; hover reveals `#F3EEEA` fill with subtle champagne-tinted text.
- **Tertiary Link Action**: Playfair italic or Plus Jakarta uppercase label paired with an animated directional hairline arrow that expands by 4px on hover.

### High-Jewelry & Gem Cards
- Encased in 1px `#E8E2DA` outlines over `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EEEA` backgrounds.
- Image areas feature a minimal, high-resolution neutral stage with an interactive "360° Loupe" badge anchored at top-right.
- Dedicated bottom grid splits into: Specimen Name (`headline-sm`), Ceylon Mine Origin Tag (e.g., *Elahera* or *Ratnapura*), Carat & Treatment indicators, and Private Acquisition value in obsidian charcoal.

### Chips & Gemological Badges
- **Origin & Cut Chips**: `#F3EEEA` background, `#141413` text, `0.25rem` radius, bordered in `#E8E2DA`.
- **Security & Lab Indicators (SSEF, Gübelin, GIA)**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with an embedded `#0D2240` micro-monogram and micro-link to official ledger validation.
- **Unheated / Natural State Chip**: Deep `#0D2240` background with `#FAF8F5` micro-text, signaling zero thermal enhancement.

### Form Inputs & Atelier Stepper
- Clean single-underline or framed box inputs with `#FAF8F5` fills and `1px` `#E8E2DA` borders. On focus, borders transition smoothly to `#C5A880` without harsh ring offsets.
- Custom Stepper for private acquisitions and bespoke commissions uses minimalist numbered Roman step counters (I, II, III, IV) connected by 1px muted champagne lines.

### Trust & Provenance Registry Module
- Vault authentication seal featuring fine-line vector geometry, cryptographic stone reference codes, and dual-signoff indicators guaranteeing conflict-free Ceylon ethical extraction.
